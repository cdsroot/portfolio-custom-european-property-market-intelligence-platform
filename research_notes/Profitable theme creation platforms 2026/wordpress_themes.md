# Economics of selling WordPress themes (data as of 2026-11-24)

Conventions used in these notes
- **[P]** primary source (platform page, company page, SEC filing, official API). **[S]** self-reported by a company or founder. **[T]** third-party estimate or secondary reporting.
- "Retrieved 2026-11-24" means the live page or API was fetched on that date for this research.
- **STALE** marks data older than 2024.
- **CALC** marks a figure computed here; inputs and limitations are shown. Nothing else is estimated.
- Fetch failures are listed in each Gaps section. Envato's author help centre (help.author.envato.com) returned HTTP 403 on every direct fetch, so the pre-2026 fee schedule is cited from a search-result snippet of that site plus secondary sources.

## 1. ThemeForest / Envato Market: fees, catalogue size, top-seller sales, thresholds, and what changed 2024-2026

### Takeaway
On 1 July 2026 Envato abolished exclusivity and moved every Market author to a flat 50% author fee (previously 12.5%-37.5% for exclusive authors and 55% for non-exclusive), which roughly halves the per-sale payout of established exclusive theme authors. ThemeForest's WordPress category still lists about 13,000 themes, but live data shows only 1,001 added in the last year and weekly sales that are extremely concentrated: Avada sold 883 copies in the latest weekly list while the 30th-ranked theme sold 8.

### Cited Findings

**Fee and commission structure**
- [P] Envato's official notice: from 1 July 2026 Envato Market moves to a "single non-exclusive model for all content"; the author fee for all authors becomes 50% (authors receive 50% of the item-price component of the list price); exclusive authors see an increase in fee (acknowledged as a reduction in earnings), non-exclusive authors drop from 55% to 50%; exclusivity requirements are removed entirely; first payout affected is 15 August 2026. Reasons given: challenging market conditions, more authors selling on multiple platforms, customers favouring subscriptions and bundles, alignment with Envato Elements. The page gives no statistics and no publication date. Retrieved 2026-11-24 — [Envato Author Hub](https://author.envato.com/hub/changes-to-envato-market-revenue-share-and-exclusivity-what-you-need-to-know/)
- [P, via search snippet only; direct fetch 403] Pre-July-2026 schedule: non-exclusive author fee 55%; exclusive author fee 12.5%-37.5%, falling as an author's sales volume grows; the fee is charged on the item price, with a separate fixed buyer fee in author-priced categories — [Envato author help: What is exclusivity?](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000471226)
- [T] ThemeREX (a ThemeForest seller, published 2026-09-04): proposed amendments were published in May 2026 and took effect 1 July 2026; "Authors who had kept up to 87.5% of each sale now keep half." - [T] Freemius (vendor of a competing sales platform; byline 2026-05-14) reports the same 1 July 2026 change and says only sellers with more than $75,000 in sales get Google Analytics for their profile — [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/envato-alternatives-wordpress-sellers/)

### Inferences
- CALC, lifetime gross at list price (sales count x price; upper/lower bounds using the lowest and highest prices evidenced above). These are NOT author earnings: list price includes Envato's buyer fee and author fee; prices changed many times over 13-14 years (ThemeREX reports Avada at $59-$60 and The7 at $39 before mid-2026); promotions, refunds, taxes and extended licences are not reflected.
  - Avada: 1,076,821 x $49 = $52.8M; x $59 = $63.5M; x $69 = $74.3M.
  - Betheme: 337,934 x $59 = $19.9M; x $69 = $23.3M.
  - The7: 337,628 x $39 = $13.2M; x $69 = $23.3M.
  - Flatsome: 271,307 x $49 = $13.3M; x $59 = $16.0M.
- CALC, weekly concentration: the 30 listed themes total 2,132 weekly sales; Avada's 883 is 41.4% of that and the top two (Avada + WoodMart, 1,207) are 56.6%. Ranks 20-30 sold 8-11 copies each. At a $59 list price, 8 sales is $472 of list-price revenue for the week before any fees. Limitation: assumes the figures on the "updated weekly" list are one-week counts (they are far below lifetime totals, but the page does not label the period).
- CALC, weekly list-price gross for Avada: 883 x $49 = $43,267.
- CALC, new-item rate: 1,001 of 12,995 WordPress themes (7.7%) were added in the last 12 months; 59 in the last month would annualise to about 708, i.e. below the trailing-year figure (one month is a small sample).
- Both third-party worked examples imply a $12 buyer fee on WordPress themes ($59 - $12 = $47; 50% = $23.50). CALC under that $12 assumption: at 50% the author receives 39.8% of a $59 list price and 41.3% of a $69 list price, before tax withholding. The $12 figure could not be confirmed on Envato's own fee page (403).
- The Block Editor compatibility count (523 of 12,995, 4.0%) versus Elementor (7,869, 60.6%) indicates ThemeForest's WordPress catalogue is overwhelmingly page-builder based rather than block/FSE based (CALC from the facet counts).
- Shutterstock's filings report Envato only inside consolidated figures, so there is no primary-source revenue series for ThemeForest after 2019.

### Gaps
- Envato's official fee schedule and buyer-fee table (help.author.envato.com articles 360000473063 and 360000472943) returned HTTP 403; the exact historical exclusive tiers by sales volume and the current buyer fee per category are not confirmed from a primary page.
- No Envato or Shutterstock publication gives ThemeForest-only revenue, WordPress-category sales, author counts, or community earnings after the December 2022 "Wrapped" post. Total community earnings as of 2026 are unpublished as far as found.
- The definition of ThemeForest's sales-tier facets (Low/Medium/High/Top Sellers) is not stated, so they cannot be converted to sales ranges.
- Exact publication dates for Avada/Betheme/The7/Flatsome are shown only as "13/14 years ago".
- No primary data on Envato Elements payouts to WordPress theme authors was found.
- The WPBay annual series could not be independently reproduced; treat as a third-party estimate with an internal inconsistency.

## 3. WordPress.org theme directory, WooCommerce Marketplace, WordPress.com

### Takeaway
WordPress.org lists 8,817 themes, of which 2,003 are tagged as block (full-site-editing) themes; the high-install positions are held by default themes, page-builder companion themes and a handful of freemium vendors. The WooCommerce Marketplace pays vendors 70% of sales; WordPress.com is reported to have stopped accepting commercial theme submissions.

- CALC: block themes are 2,003 / 8,817 = 22.7% of the WordPress.org directory; "commercial" listings (themes whose authors sell paid upgrades or support) are 2,270 / 8,817 = 25.7%.

- The freemium route is visible in the data rather than documented as a programme: the highest-install independent themes (Astra, Kadence, GeneratePress, OceanWP, Blocksy, Neve) are all free directory themes whose vendors sell a paid add-on or pro version on their own sites; WordPress.org itself takes no commission and sells nothing.

### Gaps
- WordPress.org publishes no conversion or revenue data; free-to-paid conversion rates for directory themes were not found from any primary source.
- The WooCommerce Marketplace's current theme count, theme-specific terms and vendor earnings were not found; the 70% figure applies to the Marketplace generally.
- The date and exact scope of WordPress.com's halt on commercial theme submissions, and any revenue-share terms for remaining partners, could not be confirmed from a primary Automattic source.
- Historical counts (themes added per year, block-theme count over time) were not retrieved, so growth rates are not stated.

## 4. Other marketplaces: TemplateMonster / MonsterONE, Creative Market, Mojo Marketplace

### Takeaway
TemplateMonster also dropped exclusivity in 2026 and its own help page shows a fixed 20% author commission from 1 July 2026 (a secondary source reads this the opposite way); Creative Market pays about 50% by default and lists about 2,900 WordPress themes. No current data could be retrieved for Mojo Marketplace.

### Cited Findings
- [P] TemplateMonster help page: "As of January 1, 2026, TemplateMonster no longer supports exclusive products"; all products are non-exclusive; from 1 July 2026 every product type (including WordPress) is listed at 20%; "The commission rate is fixed and does not depend on sales volume or account status." Worked example: author price $50 plus $8 customer fee = $58 storefront price; "author will receive $50 * 0.2 (20%) = $10". Retrieved 2026-11-24 — [TemplateMonster helpdesk](https://helpdesk.templatemonster.com/what-commission-will-i-get/)
- [T] CONFLICT: Freemius summarises the same change as TemplateMonster taking a flat 20% with authors receiving 80%; its Envato-alternatives table separately lists TemplateMonster at 50% and a 40%-65% range. Older TemplateMonster material describes 50%-65% for exclusive and about 40% for non-exclusive authors. The primary help page's worked example ($10 on a $50 price) supports the reading that the author receives 20% — [Freemius TemplateMonster breakdown](https://freemius.com/blog/templatemonster-marketplace-breakdown/); [Freemius alternatives](https://freemius.com/blog/envato-alternatives-wordpress-sellers/)
- [T] Creative Market (Freemius, updated 2026-10-13): shop owners earn 50% of list price by default, "commission rates do vary per shop/product"; no exclusivity; about 2,900 WordPress themes as of October 2026; theme price minimums $19 (GPL), $29 (commercial), $76 (extended commercial); 11 million members; 30% US withholding without a valid tax form; payout fees (PayPal 1% up to $10 outside the US, wire $5-$7, 2.5% FX) — [Freemius Creative Market breakdown](https://freemius.com/blog/creative-market-marketplace-breakdown/)
- [P] Creative Market's own help centre describes a separate fixed platform fee under test, in addition to commission (via search snippet) — [Creative Market support](https://support.creativemarket.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404542383259-Creative-Market-Platform-Fee)
- [T] Freemius's own pricing as a direct-sales alternative: 7% on up to $50,000 monthly gross, falling to 2.8% above $100,000, plus roughly 3.5% gateway fees (the same article's table shows a different structure, so the figures conflict internally) — [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/envato-alternatives-wordpress-sellers/)

### Inferences
- CALC from the TemplateMonster primary example: an author pricing a theme at $50 receives $10 of a $58 customer payment, 17.2% of what the buyer pays. Limitation: rests on one help-page example; the Freemius reading contradicts it.
- Across Envato (50%, from July 2026), TemplateMonster (January/July 2026) and Creative Market (about 50%), every large third-party theme marketplace reviewed has removed or never had exclusivity tiers by late 2026, and none pays a WordPress theme author more than about half of the item price by default.

### Gaps
- mojomarketplace.com returned HTTP 403 and no search result described its current operation, ownership or commission; its status in 2026 is unverified.
- MonsterONE subscription payout terms for authors were not found on the page retrieved.
- No earnings data (totals, per-author or per-item) was found for TemplateMonster, Creative Market or Mojo.
- The TemplateMonster 20% direction (author share versus marketplace share) should be confirmed in the author dashboard calculator before being relied on.

## 5. Distribution of earnings, and the effect of page builders, full site editing and AI

### Takeaway
All available evidence points to a steep power-law: live ThemeForest weekly sales fall from 883 for the top theme to single digits by rank 27, and Envato's last published figure implies an average of under $2,000 a year per earning author. Quantified, theme-specific evidence on the impact of page builders, FSE and AI builders is thin; what exists is mostly catalogue composition data and vendor commentary.

### Inferences
- CALC (STALE inputs, Dec 2022): $96M / 52,000 earning authors = about $1,846 per author per year on Envato Market. This is a mean across all Market categories (themes, code, video, audio, graphics), uses "more than" lower bounds for both inputs, and the distribution is highly skewed, so the median is necessarily lower. It pre-dates the 2026 fee change.
- CALC: 400+ Elite-milestone authors in 2022 out of 52,000+ earning authors is under 1% reaching a $75,000-lifetime-sales milestone in that year (STALE; "milestone" counts authors crossing any Elite tier that year, not the stock of Elite authors).
- CALC from the live weekly list: a theme ranked around 20th-30th in the whole WordPress category sells 8-11 copies a week; at a $59 list price and the $12 buyer fee / 50% author fee implied by third-party examples, that is roughly 8 x $23.50 = $188 to 11 x $23.50 = $259 per week to the author before tax. Limitations: the $12 buyer fee is not confirmed from Envato; several of these themes are priced differently; one week of data.
- The live facet data suggests where new theme supply is: the paid marketplace catalogue is page-builder (Elementor) based, while block themes are a 22.7% minority on WordPress.org and about 4% of ThemeForest's WordPress catalogue (CALC from counts above). No source found quantifies sales of block themes versus classic or builder-based themes.
- No source isolating the effect of AI site builders on theme sales was found; the quantified evidence relates to search traffic and new-licence sales at plugin vendors.

### Gaps
- No current (2024-2026) distribution of author earnings exists from Envato, TemplateMonster or Creative Market; the latest Envato figure is December 2022.
- No dataset of per-item weekly or monthly sales across the full ThemeForest catalogue was collected; only the top 30 weekly figures and lifetime totals for the top 15 are covered here.
- No primary or founder-reported figure quantifies the effect of Elementor, Divi, full site editing or AI site builders specifically on theme revenue. The Blocksy post that may contain such figures could not be fetched (403).
